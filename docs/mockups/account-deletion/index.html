Account controls

Your data, your decision

Delete your account

You can delete your Decorebator account in the mobile app. If you cannot sign in, request deletion by email instead.

Cancel any store subscription first

Deleting your Decorebator account does not cancel billing managed by Apple or Google. Cancel it in your store subscription settings before deleting the account.

Route 01 · fastest

Delete in the app

  1. Open Decorebator and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

The app signs you out when deletion succeeds.

Route 02 · no app access

Request deletion

Email us from the address on your account and tell us that address—nothing else. Never send a password, receipt, purchase token, or payment information.

We reply to verify account ownership before deleting anything. Verified requests are completed within the timeframe stated in our privacy policy.

What happens next

Deletion removes your learning account

  • Your profile, wordlists, and learning progress are removed from active use.
  • Some records may be retained when required for legal, security, or transaction obligations.
  • Deleting the account does not itself request a refund from Apple or Google.
