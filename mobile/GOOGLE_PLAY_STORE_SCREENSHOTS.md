## Implementation Instructions

### Magenta Replacement Workflow
Once you generate each screenshot mockup with DALL-E:

1. **Resize your app screenshot** to fit phone dimensions:
   ```bash
   convert app_screenshot.jpg -resize 275x600! screenshot_fitted.png
   ```

2. **Replace magenta with real screenshot**:
   ```bash
   convert mockup.png screenshot_fitted.png -geometry +540+130 -compose over -composite final.png
   ```

3. **Optimize for Google Play Store**:
   ```bash
   convert final.png -quality 95 -strip store_ready.png
   ```
