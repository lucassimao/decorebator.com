DECOREBATOR - AI-POWERED VOCABULARY LEARNING PLATFORM

## OVERVIEW
Decorebator is a comprehensive vocabulary learning platform that combines artificial intelligence, spaced repetition science, and interactive learning methods to help users master new languages effectively.

## CORE TECHNOLOGY
- AI Content Generation: Automatic generation for definitions, images, and audio
- Spaced Repetition: 7-box Leitner system with scientific intervals
- Multi-language Support: 7 languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese)
- Mobile Platform: iOS and Android apps
